FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not create a WakeRoutine login.

Does it work offline?

Core alarm and routine features are designed to run on-device. Purchases and restores rely on Apple services.

What permissions does it ask for?

On iOS 26, WakeRoutine asks for system alarm access. On earlier supported iOS versions, it asks for notification access to deliver scheduled alerts.

Can I still dismiss the alarm?

Yes. The system alarm always provides a stop control, and notification alerts on earlier iOS versions can be dismissed. WakeRoutine can offer a ritual action but never blocks dismissal.

Should this be my only critical alarm?

No. iOS 26 system alarms can alert through Silent mode and Focus after you grant alarm access; earlier notification-based alerts remain affected by device settings. Always use a backup when missing an alarm could cause harm or significant loss.

What's the difference between lifetime and subscriptions?

Lifetime is a one-time $59.99 purchase — you own access forever, no recurring charges. Monthly ($4.99) and yearly ($29.99) auto-renew through Apple until cancelled.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device. Lifetime has no subscription to cancel.
