Morning flow

How WakeRoutine feels in practice

1. Set your wake-up window

Pick your alarm, repeat days, and sound. Keep it simple with a quick routine or build something deeper later.

2. Begin your ritual

When the alarm rings, step into breathing, affirmations, prayer or chant, and a calming audio moment.

3. Leave with intention

Finish your routine, record the session in your streak, and carry a small intention into the rest of the day.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not create a WakeRoutine login.

Does it work offline?

Core alarm and routine features are designed to run on-device. Purchases and restores rely on Apple services.

What permissions does it ask for?

WakeRoutine currently asks for notification access so alarms can fire on time.

Can I still dismiss the alarm?

Yes. Routine-required alarms are supported, and there is also an emergency dismiss path in the ringing screen.
