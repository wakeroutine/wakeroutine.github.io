Privacy Policy

Clear privacy for a small, calm app.

WakeRoutine is designed to keep the core experience on your device. It stores routine preferences locally, uses notifications for alarms, and uses Apple and RevenueCat for purchases.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

No account required

You do not create a WakeRoutine account, and the app does not ask for a username or password.

No ads or analytics SDKs

WakeRoutine does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics tools in the current app code.

Local data

What WakeRoutine stores on your device

  • Your alarm settings, including time, active days, selected sound, snooze choice, and ritual-prompt preference.
  • Your chosen routine, enabled steps, and saved durations.
  • Your streak information, onboarding state, daily intention, custom affirmations, profile fields, quote choices, spiritual-tradition preference, and favorites.

This information is stored locally using standard iOS app storage on the device. It is used to make WakeRoutine function and is not used to build advertising profiles.

Permissions

Alarm permissions and audio behavior

Alarm delivery

On iOS 26, WakeRoutine requests system alarm access and uses Apple’s AlarmKit to schedule alarms you create. On earlier supported iOS versions, it requests notification permission and schedules local notification alerts. Permission status and alarm schedules are managed by iOS.

Audio playback

The app includes original, procedurally generated alarm tones and instrumental soundscapes. Routine audio can continue playing while the app is active.

WakeRoutine does not request microphone access in the current app code. Voice guidance is generated using Apple’s on-device speech synthesis APIs.

Purchases

How purchases are handled

WakeRoutine uses Apple’s in-app purchase system and the RevenueCat purchase-management service to load products, process and restore purchases, and confirm Pro access. Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

  • Apple processes purchase details under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat processes purchase history, product identifiers, receipts, entitlement status, and an anonymous app user identifier for app functionality, including purchase validation and restoration.
  • The developer does not link this purchase history to your name, email address, advertising identity, or WakeRoutine account; WakeRoutine has no account system.
  • WakeRoutine does not offer or locally track a free trial.
Deletion and retention

How to remove local data

Use Delete local app data in the app to reset locally stored profile, alarm, routine, streak, affirmation, quote, preference, and favorite data. Deleting the app also removes its local app container. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records as required for purchase administration, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and restoration under their respective policies.

What we do not do

No ads, no selling data, no profile building

  • No ad targeting.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the current app code.
  • No WakeRoutine account creation requirement.
