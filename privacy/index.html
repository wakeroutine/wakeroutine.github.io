Privacy Policy

Simple privacy for a small, calm app.

WakeRoutine is designed to keep the core experience on your device. It stores routine preferences locally, uses notifications for alarms, and relies on Apple for purchases.

Last updated: April 13, 2026

No account required

You do not create a WakeRoutine account, and the app does not ask for a username or password.

No ads or analytics SDKs

WakeRoutine does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics tools in the current app code.

Local data

What WakeRoutine stores on your device

  • Your alarm settings, including time, active days, selected sound, and routine-required preference.
  • Your chosen routine, enabled steps, and saved durations.
  • Your streak information, onboarding completion state, daily intention, and custom affirmations if you create any.
  • Free-use counters and other lightweight settings needed to keep the app working as expected.

This information is stored locally using standard iOS app storage on the device. It is used to make WakeRoutine function and is not used to build advertising profiles.

Permissions

Notifications and audio behavior

Notifications

WakeRoutine requests notification permission so it can schedule and deliver alarms at your chosen time.

Audio playback

The app includes local alarm sounds, ambient audio, and chants, and it can continue playback for routine-related audio behavior.

WakeRoutine does not request microphone access in the current app code. Voice guidance is generated using Apple’s on-device speech synthesis APIs.

Purchases

How subscriptions are handled

WakeRoutine uses Apple’s in-app purchase system through StoreKit to load products, process purchases, restore purchases, and confirm subscription access.

  • Apple may process purchase-related data such as product identifiers, transaction details, and receipts.
  • WakeRoutine checks current entitlements so the app can tell whether Premium is active.
  • WakeRoutine does not create a separate in-app account identifier for subscription access in the current code.
What we do not do

No ads, no selling data, no profile building

  • No ad targeting.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the current app code.
  • No WakeRoutine account creation requirement.
Contact
